# Hydraulic Press HP-200 Maintenance Manual MNT-HP200

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Hydraulic System

### 1.1 Fluid Specification
Use ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil. Operating temperature range is 40 to 55 degrees C. Do not mix oils of different brands or types.

### 1.2 Fluid Sampling and Replacement
Sample the oil every 500 operating hours. Replace the fluid when the particle count exceeds ISO 4406 code 18/16/13, water content exceeds 0.1 percent, or the acid number increases by 0.5 mg KOH/g over new oil.

### 1.3 Filter Replacement
Replace the return line filter when the differential pressure indicator is in the red zone, or every 1000 hours. Replace the pressure filter annually. Record the date on the filter housing.

## 2. Press Ram and Seals

### 2.1 Cylinder Seal Inspection
Inspect rod seals for leakage monthly. A film of oil is normal, but dripping more than 1 drop per minute requires seal replacement. Inspect the rod for scoring.

### 2.2 Ram Parallelism Check
Check ram parallelism to the bolster quarterly using dial indicators at four corners. Tolerance is 0.05 mm over the bolster length. Adjust the gibs if out of tolerance.

## 3. Safety Devices

### 3.1 Light Curtain Testing
Test the light curtain at the start of each shift with a 30 mm test rod. The press must stop within the response time displayed on the controller. Remove the machine from service if the test fails.

### 3.2 Pressure Relief Valve Setting
The relief valve is set to 210 bar and sealed. Setting changes require the Maintenance Manager approval and a logged pressure test at 110 percent of rated pressure.

## 4. Troubleshooting

### 4.1 Press Slow or Weak
Check for low oil level, a clogged suction strainer, a worn pump (flow decline over 10 percent), or internal cylinder leakage by performing a drift test.

### 4.2 Excessive Noise or Overheating
Cavitation noise suggests a blocked suction line or low oil. Overheating above 60 degrees C suggests a failing cooler, a stuck relief valve or low oil level. Shut down and investigate.
