# Final Inspection and Nonconformance Management Standard QC-STD-310

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Final Inspection

### 1.1 First Article Inspection
A first article inspection (FAI) is required at new job start-up, after tooling change or major repair, and after a process change. Production may not proceed until the FAI is approved by Quality. Every drawing characteristic is measured and recorded.

### 1.2 In-Process and Final Checks
Operators perform in-process checks per the control plan frequency. Final inspection verifies the finished product against the drawing, labeling, packaging and certificate requirements before release to shipping.

## 2. Nonconformance Management

### 2.1 Identification and Segregation
Any operator who finds a nonconforming part must stop, tag the part with a red tag, and place it in the red bin. Nonconforming parts must never be returned to the production flow.

### 2.2 Material Review Board
The Material Review Board (MRB) decides disposition: use-as-is, rework, repair, return to supplier or scrap. Use-as-is on customer-specified characteristics requires customer approval.

## 3. Corrective and Preventive Action

### 3.1 Root Cause Analysis
Use 5-Why or fishbone analysis. For repeat or critical issues use an 8D report. Containment must be implemented within 24 hours.

### 3.2 Effectiveness Verification
Verify corrective action effectiveness after 30 days of production or 3 lots, whichever is longer. Close the corrective action only when no recurrence is observed.

## 4. Records and Traceability

### 4.1 Record Retention
Inspection records are retained for 7 years, or longer if required by the customer. Records must be legible, dated and signed.

### 4.2 Lot Traceability
Each finished lot must be traceable to raw material heat or lot numbers, machine, shift and operator. Traceability labels may not be removed or altered.
