# Emergency Response and Evacuation Plan SOP-SAF-020

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Emergency Contacts and Alarms

### 1.1 Alarm Signals
A continuous siren indicates evacuation. A pulsing tone indicates shelter in place. Any employee may activate a manual pull station.

### 1.2 Emergency Numbers
Dial extension 911 from any plant phone for fire, medical or chemical emergencies. The Safety Manager is on call on extension 4402. Report all incidents, including near misses, to your supervisor before the end of the shift.

## 2. Evacuation

### 2.1 Evacuation Routes and Assembly Points
Posted evacuation maps at each exit show the nearest route. Plant 1 assembles at Assembly Point A in the north parking lot, and Plant 2 at Assembly Point B beside the loading dock. Do not use elevators.

### 2.2 Supervisor Headcount
Supervisors carry the shift roster, perform a headcount at the assembly point and report missing persons to the Incident Commander. No one re-enters the building until the all-clear is given by the fire department or Incident Commander.

## 3. Fire Response

### 3.1 Use of Fire Extinguishers
Use extinguishers only for small fires and only if trained, with an exit behind you. Follow PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. If the fire is not controlled within 30 seconds, evacuate.

### 3.2 Extinguisher Inspection
Extinguishers are visually inspected monthly and serviced annually. Inspection tags must be initialed on each check. Report any discharged, damaged or blocked extinguisher to Facilities immediately.

## 4. Medical Emergencies and Incident Reporting

### 4.1 First Aid and Injury Response
Call 911 first. Trained first aiders administer care. For amputation or severe bleeding, apply direct pressure or a tourniquet. Do not move an injured person unless in immediate danger.

### 4.2 Incident Investigation
A supervisor must file the incident report within 24 hours. Serious injuries and fatalities are reported to regulators within the legally required window by the Safety Manager. A root-cause investigation is completed within 7 days.
