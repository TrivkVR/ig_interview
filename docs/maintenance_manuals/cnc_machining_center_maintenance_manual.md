# CNC Machining Center MC-500 Maintenance Manual MNT-MC500

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Preventive Maintenance Schedule

### 1.1 Daily Checks
Check the way lubricant level, coolant concentration and level, and air pressure (minimum 5.5 bar). Wipe chips from way covers and the tool changer. Listen for abnormal noise from spindle and axes during warm-up.

### 1.2 Weekly and Monthly Tasks
Weekly: clean coolant tank filters, check the tool clamp drawbar force and inspect the chip conveyor. Monthly: check backlash on all axes, inspect way wipers and clean the electrical cabinet filters.

### 1.3 Annual Tasks
Replace hydraulic oil and filters, perform geometric accuracy check with laser interferometer and replace spindle drive belt if worn.

## 2. Spindle System

### 2.1 Spindle Warm-Up
Run the spindle warm-up program for 10 minutes ramping from 1000 rpm to 80 percent of maximum speed after any idle period exceeding 4 hours. This stabilizes bearing temperature and reduces thermal growth.

### 2.2 Spindle Bearing Lubrication
The MC-500 spindle uses oil-air lubrication. Check the oil reservoir weekly and refill with ISO VG 32 spindle oil. The air supply must be dry and filtered to 5 microns.

### 2.3 Spindle Vibration and Temperature Limits
Normal vibration is below 2.5 mm/s RMS. Investigate at 4.0 mm/s, and stop the machine at 7.0 mm/s. Spindle temperature must not exceed 45 degrees C above ambient. Alarm 2041 indicates over-temperature.

## 3. Coolant and Lubrication

### 3.1 Coolant Concentration
Maintain water-soluble coolant at 7 to 9 percent measured with a refractometer. Adjust pH to 8.5 to 9.5. Replace the coolant when bacterial odor develops or every 6 months.

### 3.2 Way Lubrication
Automatic way lubrication runs every 20 minutes. If alarm 3012 (low lube level) appears, refill with ISO VG 68 way oil. Do not use hydraulic oil.

## 4. Troubleshooting

### 4.1 Axis Servo Alarms
Alarm 410 (servo overload): check for crashes, binding in the ball screw, and way lubrication. Alarm 414 (encoder error): inspect encoder cable for damage and reseat the connector. After corrections, re-home the axis.

### 4.2 Tool Changer Faults
If the tool changer stops mid-cycle (alarm 1105), place the machine in manual mode, follow the recovery sequence, and check the pull stud and clamp air pressure. Always apply LOTO per SOP-SAF-001 before reaching into the magazine.
