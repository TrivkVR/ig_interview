# Belt Conveyor System Maintenance Manual MNT-CV10

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Inspection and Lubrication

### 1.1 Daily Walk-Down
Inspect belt tracking, damaged lagging, abnormal noise, loose guards and material buildup at transfer points. Verify that the emergency pull cords function.

### 1.2 Lubrication Schedule
Grease the head and tail pulley bearings every 250 operating hours with NLGI 2 lithium complex grease, two pumps per fitting. Do not over-grease, as this overheats bearings.

## 2. Belt and Drive

### 2.1 Belt Tracking Adjustment
If the belt drifts toward one side, adjust the return idler or tail pulley on the side the belt is moving toward in small increments, then run for 5 minutes to evaluate. Never adjust while the conveyor is running unless using a remote-adjustment system.

### 2.2 Belt Tension
Set belt tension so sag between carry idlers is no more than 2 percent of idler spacing. Over-tensioning shortens bearing and belt life.

[[PAGE 2]]

### 2.3 Gearbox and Motor
Check gearbox oil level monthly and change the oil (ISO VG 220 synthetic) every 8000 hours. Motor current must be within 10 percent of the nameplate. Thermographic scan motors quarterly, and investigate any rise above 80 degrees C.

## 3. Troubleshooting

### 3.1 Belt Slips on Drive Pulley
Check tension, worn lagging, wet or contaminated surfaces and excess load. Re-lag the drive pulley when the groove depth is below 2 mm.

### 3.2 Frequent Trips
If the overload relay trips frequently, check for jams, bearing seizure, and low supply voltage. Do not increase relay settings to avoid trips.
