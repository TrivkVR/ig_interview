# Chemical Handling and PPE Guide SOP-SAF-014

[[PAGE 1]]

## 1. Hazard Communication

### 1.1 Safety Data Sheets
A Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for every chemical on site must be available in the binder at each department entrance and in the electronic SDS library. Supervisors must ensure operators review the SDS before first use of a new chemical.

### 1.2 Container Labeling
All containers, including secondary containers such as spray bottles, must be labeled with the product name, GHS pictograms, signal word and hazard statements. Unlabeled containers must be removed from service and reported to the EHS office immediately.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment

### 2.1 Minimum PPE by Task
Safety glasses with side shields and steel-toe boots are required on the entire production floor. Machining areas additionally require hearing protection when noise exceeds 85 dBA. Welding requires a shade 10 or darker auto-darkening helmet, flame-resistant jacket and gloves.

### 2.2 Chemical-Specific PPE
Handling of solvents and degreasers requires nitrile gloves of at least 0.4 mm thickness and splash goggles. Handling of acids and caustics requires a face shield, butyl rubber gloves and a chemical-resistant apron. Replace gloves immediately after any splash or visible degradation.

## 3. Storage and Transfer

### 3.1 Flammable Liquids Storage
Flammable liquids must be stored in approved flammable cabinets. No more than 120 liters may be stored outside a cabinet per fire area. Keep cabinets closed, grounded and at least 3 meters away from ignition sources.

### 3.2 Incompatible Materials
Acids must be segregated from bases, and oxidizers from flammables and organics. Never store nitric acid with organic solvents. Secondary containment must hold 110 percent of the largest container.

### 3.3 Dispensing and Transfer
When transferring flammable liquids between metal containers, bond and ground both containers. Use only approved safety cans and pumps. Dispense in ventilated areas.

## 4. Spill Response

### 4.1 Small Spills
A small spill is under 4 liters and not highly toxic. Notify nearby personnel, wear the PPE listed in the SDS, contain with absorbent socks, absorb, bag the waste and label it as hazardous waste. Log the spill with EHS within one hour.

### 4.2 Large Spills and Emergency Eyewash
For a spill over 4 liters or any spill of unknown material, evacuate the area, call the emergency line at extension 911, and close doors. Do not attempt cleanup. For skin or eye exposure, flush at the nearest eyewash or shower for at least 15 minutes and seek medical attention.
